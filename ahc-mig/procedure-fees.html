Procedure & Fees

How a matter is taken up, and what it costs

Advisory work is free. Representation before the High Court is charged strictly by the schedule below.

Procedure

From application to first hearing

1

Submit your papers

On receipt of the requisite papers outlining the legal issue faced by the beneficiary, the matter is attended to expeditiously.

2

Hear back within 15 days

An initial response indicating the Society's decision to take up the matter is communicated to the beneficiary within 15 days of receipt of the application — including if the matter is found unfit to be pursued, with the decision explained by the assigned lawyer.

3

Meet your assigned lawyer

The beneficiary is apprised of the further steps to be taken and given the date and time of the initial interactive meeting with the lawyer assigned to the matter.

4

Advice is free; representation is charged

Advisory work is free of cost. Where the matter is to be pursued before the High Court or its Mediation and Conciliation Centre, fees are chargeable as per the scale set out below.

Fee Structure

Schedule of honorarium and expenses

Court fee payable on petitions follows the Hon'ble High Court Rules, as amended up to date, in addition to the amounts below.

  • Payable when the matter is posted for final disposal₹12,500
  • If disposed of at the initial hearing itself, payable towards honorarium only₹12,500
  • Filing caveat applications (excluding appearance when the matter is actually filed) — consolidated₹1,000
B Honorarium for Senior Advocates

  • Appearance (petitioner or respondent) when posted for final hearing — per appearance, up to a maximum of ₹20,000₹10,000
