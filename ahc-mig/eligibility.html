Eligibility

The income band that qualifies

In order to be eligible to avail the benefits of the present Scheme, the gross total income of the desirous individual should be between Rs. 6,00,000/- to Rs. 12,00,000/- per annum.

₹6,00,000–₹12,00,000Gross total annual income

This is the sole eligibility test applied by the Society. If your gross total income for the year falls within this band, you may approach the Society for advisory or representational assistance in matters before the Allahabad High Court and its Lucknow Bench.

Not sure where you stand?

Write to the Contact Persons listed under Contact Details with a brief description of your legal issue. Eligibility and the assignment of an empanelled lawyer are confirmed after the Society reviews your application — see the Procedure & Fees page for the full process and timeline.
